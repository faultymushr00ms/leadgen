## Evidence rules

- Confirmed requires three independent sources supporting the particular fact.
- Multiple NOPEC pages count as one source organization.
- A municipal page reproducing a NOPEC announcement corroborates publication locally, but does not automatically constitute independently obtained pricing evidence.
- Membership, active service, product selection and account enrollment are separate facts.
- “Not found” describes the research result, not proof that a program never existed.
- Price-validity dates are not automatically the aggregation contract's start and end.
- No residential Price to Compare is used as an aggregation rate.
- Missing fields remain open. No complete zone is declared resolved in this continuation.
