## 2. Corrections versus FORGE

1. FORGE currently assigns 10.399¢ and 9.999¢ to nearly every listed township. The product-rate progression is sourced, but the evidence reviewed does not prove that every individual township account uses the Standard Program rather than another NOPEC product. Keep the stored rates flagged unless a community notice or account bill establishes the product.

2. FORGE often stores `utility: FirstEnergy` for the townships. That is not a billable operating-company name. Retain the parent, but display:
   - `electric_utility: not resolved`
   - `utility_parent: FirstEnergy`
   - `verify_from_bill: true`

3. Ashtabula, Geneva and Conneaut may remain associated with The Illuminating Company, but this run did not establish universal municipal coverage. Address-level exceptions must remain possible.

4. Small-business inclusion is stated generally by NOPEC, but community-specific eligibility and the 700,000-kWh limit were not found for most zones. Do not mark every business eligible merely from community membership.

5. Electric-only townships must not inherit NOPEC gas status from nearby cities or the county:
   - Andover
   - Austinburg
   - Cherry Valley
   - Colebrook
   - Denmark
   - Dorset
   - Hartsgrove
   - Lenox
   - Monroe
   - Morgan
   - Orwell
   - Sheffield

6. The township rows are legitimate individual zones. They should not be collapsed into an "Ashtabula County" rate zone.

## 3. Open conflicts

### Standard Program versus community-specific product

FORGE stores 10.399¢ as current for the townships. The rate is confirmed as a NOPEC FirstEnergy-area product, but most individual township pages do not state which NOPEC product is the default for that community.

Status: **Likely – keep flagged**

Required resolution: local enrollment letter, township notice, NOPEC account lookup or customer bill naming the product and cents.

### Electric operating company

FORGE alternates between The Illuminating Company and generic FirstEnergy across Ashtabula County. Exact operating-company boundaries were not resolved in this run.

Status: **Conflict / address-dependent**

Required resolution: FirstEnergy service-territory geometry or a service-address bill.

### Gas aggregation

The community map establishes gas membership for Ashtabula, Geneva, Conneaut, Ashtabula Township, Geneva Township, Harpersfield Township, Jefferson Township and Kingsville Township. It does not establish the current gas supplier, rate or term.

Status: **Membership Confirmed; commercial terms not found**
